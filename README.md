# 🍹 Fresh Juice Shop Website

### Fresh Fruits • Fresh Juice • Fresh You

A modern, responsive, and interactive **Fresh Juice Shop Website** designed to deliver a refreshing online shopping experience. The website combines vibrant fruit visuals, animated juice cards, a looping fruit-preparation background video, and a user-friendly ordering interface.

---

## 🌟 Features

- 🎬 **Animated Video Background** — Fresh fruit cutting and juice preparation videos across all five pages.
- 🏠 **Home Page** — Attractive welcome screen with smooth animations and interactive buttons.
- 🍊 **Juice Menu** — Explore a variety of fresh juices with images, descriptions, prices, and animated product cards.
- 🛒 **Online Ordering** — Customer information form with juice selection, quantity, delivery address, and order submission.
- 📞 **Contact & About** — Shop information, opening hours, location, and contact details.
- 👋 **Thank You Page** — Creative juice-themed goodbye animation with a button to return home.
- ✨ **Smooth Animations** — Floating fruits, hover effects, transitions, and scroll animations.
- 📱 **Responsive Design** — Optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
- 🎨 **Fresh UI Design** — Vibrant fruit-inspired colors, modern typography, and attractive layouts.

2. Store it in an appropriate folder, such as `assets/videos/`.
3. Configure the background video to autoplay, loop, and remain muted.
4. Use `object-fit: cover` to fill the screen.
